२. इन्टरनेट

ShuddhaType सँग INTERNET अनुमति छैन। यो Android ले नै लागू गर्ने प्रतिबन्ध हो — अनुमति नभएको एपले इन्टरनेटमा जोडिन सक्दैन। त्यसैले तपाईंले लेखेको कुनै पनि कुरा यन्त्रबाट बाहिर जाने कुनै उपाय छैन।

तपाईं यो आफैँ जाँच्न सक्नुहुन्छ: फोनको सेटिङ → Apps → ShuddhaType → Permissions।

३. तपाईंले टाइप गरेको पाठ

किबोर्डले काम गर्न तपाईंले थिचेका कुञ्जी पढ्नुपर्छ — त्यो किबोर्डको स्वभाव हो। तर त्यो पाठ केवल तपाईंकै यन्त्रभित्र, तपाईं लेख्दै गरेको एपमा पठाइन्छ। ShuddhaType ले त्यसलाई कहीँ सङ्कलन गर्दैन, सुरक्षित राख्दैन, र कतै पठाउँदैन।

शब्द मिलाउने काम एपभित्रैको शब्दकोशले गर्छ। कुनै सर्भर, कुनै AI सेवा प्रयोग गरिँदैन।

एप अनइन्स्टल गर्दा यी सबै मेटिन्छन्।

Privacy Policy (English)

In short ShuddhaType collects no data, stores no data, and sends nothing anywhere. The app does not even hold the permission needed to use the internet.

1. What we collect

Nothing. ShuddhaType collects no personal information, no typed text, no contacts, no photos, no location, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, and no usage analytics.

2. Internet access

ShuddhaType does not declare the INTERNET permission. Android itself enforces this: an app without that permission cannot open a network connection. There is therefore no route by which anything you type could leave your device. You can verify this yourself under Settings → Apps → ShuddhaType → Permissions.

3. Text you type

A keyboard must read the keys you press in order to work. That text is delivered only to the app you are writing in, on your own device. ShuddhaType does not collect it, retain it, or transmit it. Word suggestions come from a dictionary bundled inside the app — no server and no AI service is contacted.

4. Data stored on your device

Your shortcuts, your own letter templates, your theme choice and your recently used emoji are stored on your phone only, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

5. Sensitive fields

In password and other sensitive fields, the keyboard shows no suggestions and does not remember recent emoji.

6. Third parties

No advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

7. Children

ShuddhaType collects no information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future feature requires internet access, this page will be updated and you will be told clearly before that feature is used.
